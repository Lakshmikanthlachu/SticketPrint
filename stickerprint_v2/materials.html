Materials & Finishes

Choose the perfect stock, finish & coating

Every project is different — explore our full range of vinyl, paper and specialty finishes to find the right match.

Matte Vinyl

A soft, non-reflective finish that resists fingerprints and glare — great for minimalist branding.

Glossy Vinyl

High-shine finish that makes colors pop; the most popular choice for product labels.

Holographic

Rainbow-shifting foil effect that catches the light — a favorite for limited drops.

Clear / Transparent

Print-only-where-needed film for a seamless, label-free look on glass or plastic.

Kraft Paper

Natural, recyclable texture for an organic, artisanal brand feel.

Glitter Vinyl

Sparkling texture finish, popular for events, merch and seasonal packaging.

BOPP Waterproof

Industrial-grade waterproof film built for fridges, freezers and bathrooms.

Static Cling

No-adhesive removable film for windows and glass — leaves zero residue.
