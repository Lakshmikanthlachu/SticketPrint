    File Requirements

    Guidelines for print-ready artwork

    Vector formats preferred

    AI, EPS, PDF or SVG give the crispest die-cut edges and scale perfectly.

    Raster minimum 300 DPI

    PNG or JPEG files must be at least 300 DPI at final print size — no exceptions for sharp print.

    Add a 0.125″ bleed

    Extend background artwork 1/8 inch past the cut line so there’s no white edge after trimming.

    Outline all fonts

    Convert text to outlines/paths to avoid missing font substitutions during prepress.

    Use CMYK color mode

    RGB files are converted automatically, but supplying CMYK gives the most accurate proof match.

    Keep text inside the safe zone

    Keep important text/logos at least 0.1″ inside the cut line to avoid trimming.
