About Us

Print people, obsessed with the details

We’re a team of press operators, designers and packaging nerds who believe great products deserve great labels.

Our Mission

Make premium print accessible to every brand, at every size

We started Stikko because small studios and growing brands deserve the same production quality as national retailers — without the enterprise price tag or six-week lead times.

Today our 10,000 sq.ft. facility runs six dedicated press lines producing everything from single custom stickers to million-unit label programs, all backed by a 100% reprint guarantee.

Our Story

From garage cutter to national print partner

2014

Founded in a garage

Started with a single desktop cutter and a handful of Etsy customers.

2017

First in-house press

Brought digital printing in-house to control quality and turnaround.

2020

10,000 sq.ft. facility

Moved into a dedicated production facility with 6 press lines.

2023

Enterprise program launched

Began serving CPG & retail brands with API ordering and QC programs.

2026

180,000+ orders shipped

Serving 8,500+ active brands across 35 countries.
